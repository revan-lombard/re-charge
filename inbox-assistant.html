    Questions

    Questions.

    Does it send emails by itself?

    No. It writes the reply on your page; a person checks it, copies it into your email and presses send. Anything it isn’t sure of is marked for you to fill in.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    Then the person checking it fixes it before it goes out, and tells us, and we correct it so it doesn’t happen again. We also review a sample every month.

    Which email does it work with?

    Any of them: Gmail, Outlook or whatever you use, because you paste the email into your page. On Microsoft 365 we can also have the drafts waiting in your inbox, with no pasting at all.

    Will it sound like us?

    Yes. We set it up from your own past replies, your tone and your sign-off, and it answers in the language the customer wrote in, including Afrikaans.

    What happens to our data?

    It’s used only to draft your replies, under POPIA. We don’t keep the emails you paste, only a one-line note of what each was about. If you don’t keep it, we switch your page off and delete it. Privacy

    When do we pay?

    Only at the end. We set it up and your team uses it for two weeks at no cost. If it’s saving your team at least 5 hours a week, measured the way we agreed on day one, you pay the R9,500 setup and the R2,950 monthly plan starts. If it isn’t, you pay nothing. It needs the access and information we agreed on day one.

    We get fewer than 20 emails a day. Is it worth it?

    Probably not yet, and we’ll say so. A few saved replies or a shared template might do the job. If your volume grows, we can look again.
