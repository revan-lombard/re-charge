# Phone cover-screen QR (Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 6)

Your phone's cover screen as your business card: people scan the QR and land on **re-charge.co.za/card**, which shows:
- the floating 3D logo
- Book a call
- WhatsApp
- Save contact

- `re-charge-qr-zflip6-cover-720x748.png`: the cover screen's own size. Use this one.
- `re-charge-qr-zflip6-cover-1080x1122.png`: a sharper copy, if the phone asks for a bigger image.
- `…-720x748-dark-panel.png`: a black screen with the code on a white panel. It looks dark on the phone but scans on every phone. **Recommended** if you want the dark look.
- `…-720x748-inverted.png`: a white code on black. Newer phone cameras (recent iPhones, Samsung, Google Lens) read it, but some older phones and scanner apps can't. A basic QR reader failed on it in testing.

It's the **same QR code** as the printed card.

## Setting it as the cover-screen wallpaper

1. Send the 720×748 image to your phone (WhatsApp it to yourself as a *document* so it isn't compressed, or use Google Drive).
2. Settings → **Cover screen** → **Wallpaper** → **Gallery** → pick the image.
3. Pinch until the whole QR code is visible. It sits slightly low on purpose, so the clock at the top doesn't cover it.

## For reliable scanning

- Turn the cover screen brightness up when you show it.
- Keep the clock style small, or move it to the top edge. Nothing should overlap the QR code.
- If someone's camera struggles, tilt the phone slightly away from overhead lights to stop reflections.
