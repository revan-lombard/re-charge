# Google profile setup (R450): selling it

**The product:** R450 once-off, paid when it's done and they've checked it. It covers:
- the profile claimed or created and verified;
- the right category and area, hours, phone, WhatsApp and website;
- services and prices, and a description written for them;
- photos, logo and cover, and their first 2 posts;
- a review QR card.

If they build a website with you within 90 days, the R450 comes off it (take it off the website quote).

Images (in this folder):
- `whatsapp-status-google-setup.png`: Status. Before/after listing, "Reply: Google".
- `whatsapp-chat-google-setup-1080x1350.png`: send in a chat.

Link to it: `https://re-charge.co.za/services?google=1` opens the booking pop-up straight away.
`https://re-charge.co.za/services#google` shows what's included.

Can customers find you on Google Maps? We'll set up your Google profile properly for R450, and
you pay once it's done. Reply "Google".

## When someone replies "Google"
Great! What's your business called, and are you on Google Maps already (even an old listing)? I'll
take it from there. You only pay the R450 once it's done and you've checked it.

## In the panel
- **Website bookings:** they arrive as a lead with the next step *"Google profile setup (R450): get their
  hours, services and photos"*. From there: **Message them**, then **Start the work** (adds the R450), then
  **Make their review card**, **Ask for payment** and **It's done**. After that, **Offer a website**.
- **Prospects with fewer than 10 Google reviews** also get an **Offer Google setup** button next to the
  usual intro.
- **Review cards:** the panel page `admin/review-card.html` makes a printable A6 card and a WhatsApp image
  with a QR code for any business's review link. On a client's page, **Review card** fills it in.

Your own review card (for Re-Charge clients) is in `../review-card/`.
