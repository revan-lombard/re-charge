# Future idea: the course (parked, 2026-10-14)

Not now. Written down so it isn't lost.

**The idea:** a course teaching people how to start an AI integration business like Re-Charge.

**How it fits with the content:**
- **"Let's Build" videos (free):** follow-along builds, where people watch a real integration come together and can set up the same thing for themselves. They show what it does and the steps to follow, not a deep explanation of how it's made.
- **The course (paid):** for people who want to learn it properly and in depth: the method (Find → Prove → Build), pricing and quoting, measuring results, contracts, finding clients, and running it as a business.

The free videos build trust and an audience. The course is for the people who want to go further.

**Why later is right:**
- The course is far more convincing once Re-Charge has real client results to teach from: "here's how I got to N clients", not theory.
- The founding-client case studies become the course's examples.
- Selling a course on how to run a business before that business has clients is the claim-without-proof the rest of the site avoids.

**When to revisit:** after the first 3–5 clients have measured results.

**Material we already have to build from:**
- METHOD.md (the method and commercial model)
- the contract set
- pricing logic
- the outreach templates
- the panel workflows
