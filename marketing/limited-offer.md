# Limited offer: posts and ad copy

Wording rule: say **"our next 10 website clients"**, never "first 10" or "founding". It reads as an
established studio running a promotion, not a new one looking for its first customers.

Images (in this folder):
- `limited-offer-status-1080x1920.png`: WhatsApp Status, Instagram / Facebook Stories
- `limited-offer-feed-1080x1350.png`: Facebook / Instagram feed
- `limited-offer-square-1080x1080.png`: Facebook groups, LinkedIn, anywhere square
- `limited-offer-google-offer-1200x900.png`: Google Business Profile → Add update → Offer
- `limited-offer-link-1200x630.png`: link previews, Facebook / LinkedIn landscape posts, email

Videos (`/assets/video/ads/`): pair the 15-second cut with the feed image in the same campaign.

Link to use everywhere (tracked in Numbers):
`https://re-charge.co.za/free-mockup?src=offer`
