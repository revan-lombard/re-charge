What this looks like

A process, a system, a number.

We don’t sell AI. We take one process that eats your team’s time, build the system that does the repetitive part, and measure the difference.

Enquiries
Problem

Staff spend around 15 hours a week reading enquiries and writing replies.

Solution

AI reads each incoming enquiry, extracts the requirements, checks your knowledge base and prepares the response for a person to approve.

Result

Response preparation from ~15 minutes to ~3 minutes.

Knowledge
Problem

Policies, prices and procedures live in shared drives, PDFs and old emails — or in the head of the one person everyone interrupts.

Solution

The documents your staff search for manually become one integrated knowledge base. They ask in plain language and get the answer with its source linked.

Result

Finding an answer from ~10 minutes of searching to under 1 minute.

Admin
Problem

Invoices, forms and orders arrive as attachments and get retyped into your accounting or job system.

Solution

AI reads the document, extracts the fields, checks them against your records and posts them to your software — flagging anything unusual for review.

Result

Capture time from ~6 minutes to ~1 minute per document.

These are examples of the results we target. Every engagement starts by measuring your baseline, and every result we report is measured against it.

The Re-Charge Method

Seven phases. Nothing goes to production that hasn’t proved itself.

  1. 1Discovery

    Map the people, processes, software, data, bottlenecks and decision points. Measure the baseline.

  2. 2Opportunity mapping

    Score every opportunity on time saved, revenue, difficulty, data, risk and adoption.

  3. 3Proof of concept

    Build the smallest useful version, on real work.

  4. 4ROI validation

    Before vs after, on the same measure. Go, adjust, or stop.

  5. 5Production

    Integrate properly with your software, with logging and safe fallbacks.

  6. 6Adoption

    Train the people who use it, and adjust to how they actually work.

  7. 7Optimisation

    Monitor, evaluate and improve. A report every month.

Questions

Straight answers.

What happens in a process review?

A free 30-minute call. You tell us where your team’s time goes; we tell you honestly whether AI is the answer, which process we’d look at first, and what discovery would involve. If AI isn’t the right fix, we’ll say so.

Why pay for discovery?

Because it’s real work: we map the process, measure the baseline and score every opportunity, and you keep the map whether or not you build with us. It’s R7,500, and it comes off your implementation in full if you go ahead within 60 days.

Our data is messy. Is that a problem?

It’s normal. Data availability is one of the six things we score. If it’s not ready, we tell you what to fix first rather than building something that can’t work.

What does Operations actually cover?

Monitoring, the model and API costs up to your plan’s allowance, maintenance and security updates, usage monitoring, regular evaluation of the system’s accuracy, support to a defined response time, hours for workflow improvements and small integrations, and a monthly report that leads with what improved and by how much.

What about our data and privacy?

Your data stays in accounts you own, is used only to run your system, and is handled under POPIA. Where a wrong answer could cost you, the system drafts and a person approves.
