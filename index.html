Questions

Straight answers.

How much does a project cost?

Quick builds and one-page websites start from R1,000, a business website from R2,500, and custom websites from R4,500. Dashboards and automation start from R2,000, AI integrations from R3,500 and custom tools from R4,500. You see an indicative estimate before you pay, and a fixed quote once we've reviewed your project.

What is the R500 deposit for?

It starts the review and reserves your slot. We confirm the final scope, price and timeline first — and the R500 is fully refundable until you approve that quote. Approve it and the R500 comes off your project price; walk away for any reason and you get it back.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's completely fine, and it's a normal way to start. Tell us what you're currently doing, what's frustrating you, or what you'd like to improve. We'll work out what could be built to solve it. You don't need any technical terms.

How does hosting work?

A website is only live while it's hosted, so some hosting is required — separate from the one-off build. Ongoing sites take an annual plan: Hosting R400/year, Hosting & Care R600/year (small updates included) or Business Care R2,400/year. Event or once-off sites (a wedding, a launch) are hosted only for the period you need. You own your domain and can host elsewhere if you prefer.

Do you use templates?

We build around what your business needs, using reusable components and modern development methods so we can deliver quickly and keep prices low. You're buying the result and the speed, not a page-builder you'll have to fight later.

What about AI running costs?

Third-party AI/API usage is billed separately, based on actual usage, and we explain it before we build anything. We never bundle unlimited AI usage into a fixed project price.

Can I start small and grow later?

Yes — that's the idea. A R1,000 website is an entry point, not the end product. Many clients add a dashboard, an automation or a custom tool later, once the first thing is paying off.
