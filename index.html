Questions

Straight answers.

How quickly will you reply, and how quickly is my site live?

We reply within an hour, every day from 7am to 9pm (messages after 9pm get a reply by 8am). Once you approve the design and we have your logo, photos and prices, your website is live within 7 days, or your first month of Care is free. The details

How much does a project cost?

One-page websites start from R1,000, a business website from R2,000, and custom websites from R4,500. Dashboards and automation start from R7,500, AI assistants from R5,900 and custom tools from R17,500. You see an indicative estimate before you pay, and a fixed quote once we’ve reviewed your project.

When do I pay the deposit?

Only once you've seen and accepted your fixed quote. Tell us what you need (free), we send you a fixed quote online with the scope, price and timeline, and if you're happy you accept it and pay the deposit by card. It comes off your project price, and the balance is due on completion. Don't like the quote? Don't accept it — you haven't paid anything.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's completely fine, and it's a normal way to start. Tell us what you're currently doing, what's frustrating you, or what you'd like to improve. We’ll work out what could be built to solve it. You don't need any technical terms.

What about AI running costs?

Third-party AI/API usage is billed separately, based on actual usage, and we explain it before we build anything. We never bundle unlimited AI usage into a fixed project price.
