Questions

Straight answers.

How much does a project cost?

Websites start from R2,500, dashboards and automation from R3,500, AI solutions from R5,000 and custom software from R10,000. Those are starting points, not fixed prices. Once we understand what you need, you receive a fixed quote before any development begins.

How quickly can you deliver?

Typical targets: simple websites in 2–5 business days, dashboards in 3–7 business days, small automations in 3–10 business days, simple AI integrations in 1–3 weeks. Larger applications are quoted individually. These are target timeframes, not guaranteed delivery dates — your quote states the estimate for your project.

What if I don't know exactly what I need?

That's the normal starting point. Describe the problem or the idea in plain language — "we lose track of quotes", "I want a site people can book from". We'll work out the simplest practical solution and send you a clear scope, price and timeframe.

Do you use templates?

No. Re-Charge builds around your requirements rather than fitting your business into a template. Modern development methods allow us to deliver custom solutions faster and more efficiently than traditional agencies, which is what keeps the prices where they are.

Do I own what you build?

Your quotation clearly defines ownership of the source code, designs, your data, third-party components, licences and any AI or API accounts. In most projects you own the delivered solution and all of your data once the final payment is made. Re-Charge keeps the right to reuse its own generic, non-client-specific components.

What about hosting, domains and AI costs?

Third-party costs such as hosting, domains, paid software and AI/API usage are identified up front. They're either paid directly by you or listed as a clearly defined additional cost in the quote. They're never hidden in the project price.

How does payment work?

Projects below R10,000 are usually 50% deposit and 50% before final handover. Larger projects are typically 40–50% deposit with the balance split across project milestones. The exact structure is confirmed in your quotation, and development starts after the deposit is received.

What happens if I want changes later?

Every quote includes a defined number of revision rounds. Anything outside the original scope becomes a change request, which we quote separately before doing the work. After launch, optional monthly support covers minor updates; larger changes are quoted as small projects.
