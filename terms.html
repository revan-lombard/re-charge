Terms & deposit

How our projects work.

How projects, deposits, hosting and ownership work — in plain language.

Last updated: September 2026

Plain-language summary — not the final legal terms. Formal Terms & Conditions come with your quotation and take precedence. Unclear on anything? Ask before you pay a deposit.

1. Scope

Every project has a fixed scope, agreed in writing before we build — what's included, revision rounds and delivery. Anything outside it is a change request.

2. The R500 deposit

Starts the review and reserves your slot. It doesn't accept the project at the estimate — we confirm scope, price and timeline first, and the deposit is fully refundable until you approve that quote.

  • You approve the quote → the deposit comes off your total.
  • You decide not to go ahead, for any reason (including the price) → fully refunded.
  • We can't take the project on → refunded.
  • Larger projects may use a 30–50% deposit instead, set in your quote.

3. Payment & refunds

The balance is due as set out in your quote — before handover for standard projects, or across milestones for larger ones. Once work has started, amounts paid cover work done and are generally non-refundable, except where the Consumer Protection Act requires. Hit a snag? Talk to us.

How you pay:

  • The R500 deposit is paid securely by card via a Yoco payment link.
  • The balance is sent as a Yoco invoice you can pay by card, or by EFT / bank transfer if you prefer — your choice.
  • Card details are handled entirely by Yoco; we never see or store them. You get a receipt for every payment.

4. Revisions & changes

Each project includes a set number of revision rounds — give feedback in one batch per round. Anything beyond that, or outside scope, is quoted as a change request and only done once you approve it.

5. Your part

Fast delivery needs your content, images, access and timely feedback. Missing pieces can move the date; we'll flag it when something's holding things up.

6. Third-party costs & AI

Domains, paid software, payment providers and AI/API usage are third-party costs, identified up front. AI/API usage is billed on actual use — never "unlimited" in a fixed price.

7. Hosting, domains & ownership

  • A site is only live while it's hosted, so some hosting is required — ours (billed yearly, cancellable) or your own.
  • You own your domain — we can manage the tech, but you're the registrant and can transfer it away.
  • On full payment you own the delivered solution and your data; we reuse only our own generic components and know-how.
  • Care covers small updates within its allowance; new pages, features and redesigns are quoted separately. Unused time doesn't roll over.

8. Warranty, cancellation & support

We fix genuine bugs in delivered work free for a reasonable period after launch; new features and content changes go under a Care plan or a separate quote. Cancel anytime — work already done is payable and the deposit follows section 2. If a project goes silent for a long stretch, we may pause or close it and invoice for work completed.

9. Portfolio & privacy

We may feature finished work unless you ask us not to; we never publish your confidential data, and concept/demo work is never shown as a real client. Files you submit are used only to deliver your project — validated, size-limited, sent over HTTPS and kept out of search engines. See the Privacy Policy.
