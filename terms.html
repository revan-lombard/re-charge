Terms & deposit

How our projects work.

How projects, deposits, hosting and ownership work — in plain language.

Last updated: September 2026

Plain-language summary — not the final legal terms. Formal Terms & Conditions come with your quotation and take precedence. Unclear on anything? Ask before you pay a deposit.

1. Scope

Every project has a fixed scope, agreed in writing before we build — what's included, revision rounds and delivery. Anything outside it is a change request.

2. The deposit

You only pay it after you've seen and accepted a fixed quote. Telling us what you need, getting the quote and getting a free mockup cost nothing.

  • Tell us what you need (Project Builder or free mockup) → free.
  • We send you a fixed quote online — scope, price and timeline.
  • You accept it online and pay the deposit by card → it comes off your total and we start building.
  • You decide not to go ahead (including because of the price) → just don't accept the quote; you haven't paid anything.
  • We can't take the project on after you've paid → refunded.
  • Larger projects may use a 30–50% deposit instead, set in your quote.

3. Payment & refunds

The balance is due as set out in your quote — before handover for standard projects, or across milestones for larger ones. Once work has started, amounts paid cover work done and are generally non-refundable, except where the Consumer Protection Act requires. Hit a snag? Talk to us.

How you pay:

  • The deposit is 40% of your quoted total, rounded to the nearest R100 and never less than R500 — or the full amount where the job is smaller than that. It is paid securely by card through Yoco when you accept your quote online, and comes off your total.
  • The balance is sent as a Yoco invoice you can pay by card, or by EFT / bank transfer if you prefer — your choice.
  • Card details are handled entirely by Yoco; we never see or store them. You get a receipt for every payment.

4. Revisions & changes

Each project includes a set number of revision rounds — give feedback in one batch per round. Anything beyond that, or outside scope, is quoted as a change request and only done once you approve it.

5. Your part

Fast delivery needs your content, images, access and timely feedback. Missing pieces can move the date; we'll flag it when something's holding things up.

6. Third-party costs & AI

Domains, paid software, payment providers and AI/API usage are third-party costs, identified up front. AI/API usage is billed on actual use — never "unlimited" in a fixed price.

7. Hosting, domains & ownership

  • A site is only live while it's hosted, so some hosting is required — ours (billed yearly, cancellable) or your own.
  • You own your domain — we can manage the tech, but you're the registrant and can transfer it away.
  • On full payment you own the delivered solution and your data; we reuse only our own generic components and know-how.
  • Care covers small updates within its allowance; new pages, features and redesigns are quoted separately. Unused time doesn't roll over.
  • Website plans: Hosting R50/month or R500/year, Care R150/month or R1,500/year. Clients on the earlier AI Care (R550/month) or Partner retainer (R6,500/month) plans keep them, at the price they signed up at, until they choose to change. Paying yearly is 2 months free. Monthly plans are paid by card link each month or by bank stop order, and can be cancelled with a month's notice. A new website's quote includes the first year of Care; you can switch plans or billing at renewal. Existing clients keep their price until they change plans.
  • Pay monthly: if you choose a pay-monthly website, there's no deposit: you pay the first month when you accept the quote, then the same amount each month for the agreed term (usually R249 a month for 12 months), after which it becomes a normal Care plan (R150 a month). The minimum is the full term. If you stop paying before it ends, you can keep the site by paying the rest of the term in one go, or we take the site offline. Your domain is yours either way.
  • Footer credit: while a small "Website by Re-Charge" link stays in your site's footer, your plan is R100 a year (R10 a month) cheaper. You can ask for it to be removed at any time; the discount then ends at your next renewal.
  • Referrals: a business you refer (through your referral link, or by telling us) gets R250 off its website. When it pays its balance and goes live, your next year of Care is free. It's added to your plan's renewal date and can't be exchanged for cash.

7a. AI implementation & operations

  • Starting prices. Fixed-scope builds start from R1,000 (websites), R2,000 (dashboards and automations), R3,500 (AI solutions) and R4,500 (custom software). Starting prices cover the simplest useful version; your proposal sets the actual scope and price before development, and ongoing costs (hosting, subscriptions, AI usage, support) are listed in it.
  • Stages, for larger AI process work. Automating a whole process runs in stages: a free process review, a fixed-price discovery (R7,500), a proof of concept (from R18,500) and production implementation (from R45,000). Each paid stage has a written scope and a fixed price, starts with a 40% deposit and ends with a decision to continue or stop. The discovery fee is credited in full against implementation if you go ahead within 60 days.
  • Operations. Operate R4,500/month, Optimise R9,500/month, Partner R18,500/month. Operations starts at go-live, has a 3-month minimum and then runs month to month with a month’s notice. It includes monitoring, model/API costs up to the plan’s allowance, maintenance, security updates, usage monitoring, evaluation, support to the service levels, the plan’s improvement hours and a monthly ROI report.
  • Service levels. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00 SAST, excluding public holidays. Response times (Operate / Optimise / Partner): critical — next business day / 4 business hours / 1 business hour; high — next business day / 4 / 2 business hours; normal — 2 business days / next business day / 4 business hours. A response is a person acknowledging the issue with a first assessment; it is not a guaranteed fix time. Third-party AI providers’ uptime is outside our control; we monitor them and design your system to fall back safely.
  • Allowances. Model/API use above the plan’s allowance is billed at cost; we warn you at 80%. Unused improvement hours don’t roll over, except on Partner (one month).
  • Priced separately. Change requests beyond the plan’s hours R850/hour, quoted before work starts. New functionality is a separate project with its own fixed quote. Consulting R1,250/hour (half-day workshop R4,500). Emergency support outside business hours R1,650/hour, 1-hour minimum.
  • Results. We measure a baseline before building and the same measure after, and report it monthly. Examples of results on this site are examples, not guarantees; your result depends on your process, data and adoption.
  • Data. Your data is processed only to run your system, in accounts you own where possible, under POPIA. We tell you which third-party providers process it before anything is built.

8. Our two promises

  • A reply within an hour, every day from 7am to 9pm. Message us on WhatsApp, by email or through a form on this site, and a person (not an automatic email) replies within 60 minutes. Messages after 9pm get a reply by 8am the next morning.
  • Your website live within 7 days of approving the design, or your first month of Care is free. The 7 days start when you tell us in writing (WhatsApp or email) that you're happy with the design, and we have what we asked you for: your logo, photos, wording and prices, and access to your domain if you already have one.
  • The clock pauses while we wait on you (changes you ask for after approving, or answers we need) and for things outside our control, like a domain registrar or a transfer from your old host. Larger builds such as online shops, booking systems and portals have their own promised date in the quote, with the same free month if we miss it.
  • The free month is added to your Care plan (your renewal or next payment moves a month later). It can't be exchanged for cash.

9. Warranty, cancellation & support

We fix genuine bugs in delivered work free for a reasonable period after launch; new features and content changes go under a Care plan or a separate quote. Cancel anytime — work already done is payable, as set out in section 3. If a project goes silent for a long stretch, we may pause or close it and invoice for work completed.

10. Portfolio & privacy

We may feature finished work unless you ask us not to; we never publish your confidential data, and concept/demo work is never shown as a real client. Files you submit are used only to deliver your project — validated, size-limited, sent over HTTPS and kept out of search engines. See the Privacy Policy.
