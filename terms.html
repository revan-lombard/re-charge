How our projects work.

A plain-language summary of how Re-Charge projects, deposits, hosting and ownership work, so there are no surprises.

Last updated: September 2026

Please note: this is a plain-language summary of how we work, not final legal terms. Formal Terms & Conditions will be provided with your quotation and take precedence. If anything here is unclear, ask before you pay a deposit.

1. Projects & scope

Every project has a fixed scope agreed in writing before development begins. The scope lists what's included, the number of revision rounds and the delivery expectation. Anything not in the scope is a change request.

2. The R500 deposit

The R500 project deposit starts the review process and is credited toward your final project price. Paying it does not automatically accept the project at the indicative estimate — we review your requirements and confirm the final scope, price and timeline first.

  • If we take the project on, the deposit comes off your total.
  • If we're unable to take the project on after review, the deposit is refunded.
  • For larger projects, a percentage deposit (generally 30–50%) may apply instead, confirmed in your quote.

3. Payment

After the deposit, the remaining balance is due as set out in your quote — typically before final handover for standard projects, or split across milestones for larger ones. Development on each stage begins once its payment terms are met.

4. Refunds

The deposit is refundable if we decline the project. Once development has started, amounts already paid cover work already done and are generally non-refundable, except where required by the Consumer Protection Act. If a problem arises, talk to us — we'll always try to find a fair outcome.

5. Revisions & changes

Each project includes a defined number of revision rounds. Feedback is best given in one batch per round. Work beyond the agreed revisions, or outside the original scope, is quoted as a change request and only done once you approve it.

6. Your responsibilities

To keep delivery fast, you agree to provide the content, images, access and information the project needs, and to give feedback within a reasonable time. Delays caused by missing content or feedback can move the delivery date; we'll always tell you when something is holding things up.

7. Third-party services & AI usage

Domains, paid software, payment providers and AI/API usage are third-party costs. They're identified up front and either paid directly by you or added as a clearly defined cost. AI/API usage is billed on actual usage and is never included as "unlimited" in a fixed price.

8. Domains, hosting & ownership

  • You own your domain. We can manage the technical side, but you remain the rightful registrant and can transfer the domain away if you leave.
  • Hosting and Care plans are billed yearly and can be cancelled; on cancellation we help you move to another provider.
  • On full payment, you own the delivered solution and your data. Re-Charge retains the right to reuse its own generic, non-client-specific components, templates and know-how.

9. Hosting & Care plans

Care plans cover small content updates and technical maintenance within the stated allowance. New pages, new functionality, redesigns and integrations are quoted separately. Unused time doesn't roll over between quarters.

10. Warranty & support

We fix genuine bugs in delivered work at no charge for a reasonable period after launch. Changes in third-party services, new feature requests and content changes are handled under a Care plan or quoted separately.

11. Cancellation & abandoned projects

You can cancel a project at any time; amounts covering completed work are payable, and the deposit is handled per section 2. If a project goes silent for an extended period without response, we may pause or close it and invoice for work completed.

12. Portfolio use

We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to. We never publish your confidential business data, and clearly labelled demo/concept work is never presented as a real client project.

13. Privacy

Information and files you submit are used only to review and deliver your project. Uploads are validated and size-limited, submissions are protected in transit (HTTPS), and project information is kept out of search engines. See how we handle data before relying on it for confidential material.
