Questions restaurants ask

Straight answers.

Can I update the menu and specials myself?

Yes. Price and specials changes are included on the Care plan, or we set it up so you can edit the menu from your phone.

Do you charge commission on orders?

No. WhatsApp ordering has no per-order fees. Online payments go through Yoco at their normal card rate — nothing to us.

Can it show today's specials?

Yes — a specials block at the top of the menu that you can change in a minute.
