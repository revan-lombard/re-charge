Share images

Images for clients to post on Facebook, Instagram or their WhatsApp status. Every one they post shows their customers your work.

Leave a box empty to leave it off. The panel fills these in from Google Analytics and Search Console where they're connected.

How to use it

Download it, then send it to the client on WhatsApp with the launch (or year-end) message from the panel. Ask them to post it and tag you. The image is 1080 × 1350, which suits Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp status.
