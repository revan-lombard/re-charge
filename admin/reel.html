Before/after reel

A 20-second vertical video for Status, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook: their Google listing before, their new website after, and your call to action. Nothing is uploaded: it's made right here in your browser.

Getting the screenshots
  1. New site: open it on your phone and take a scrolling (full-page) screenshot. On a computer: Chrome → right-click → Inspect → phone icon → ⋮ → Capture full size screenshot.
  2. Before: search their name on Google Maps on your phone and screenshot the listing. Take it before you update their profile.
  3. Ask the client if you may post it (most are happy to be shown off).

Keep this tab open while it records. Add music in Instagram or TikTok when you post.
