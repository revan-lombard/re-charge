Review card

A card with a QR code that opens the Google review box. Print it for the counter, or send the image to happy customers on WhatsApp.

Where to find the review link
  1. Open the business's Google profile (search its name on Google while signed in as the owner).
  2. Tap Ask for reviews (or Get more reviews) and copy the link.

Scan the QR with your phone before printing lots of them.
