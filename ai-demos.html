01 · Meeting attendant

Notes, decisions and actions, before the meeting ends.

The work
Someone takes notes instead of taking part, then spends 20 minutes writing them up.
We measure
Minutes per write-up · actions with an owner and a date
Good for
Teams with regular meetings, client calls or site visits.
Limits
Needs everyone’s consent to record; strong accents and crosstalk reduce accuracy, so someone checks the notes before they go out.
Sample data

    Notes ready the moment the call ends, emailed to everyone who attended, with each action linked to the moment it was agreed. Click any item above to see where it came from.

            07 · Document answers (RAG)

            Answers from your documents, with the page they came from.

            The work
            Searching shared drives and PDFs, or interrupting the one person who knows the policy.
            We measure
            Minutes to find an answer · questions to senior staff
            Good for
            Teams with policies, manuals, price lists or procedures spread across files.
            Limits
            Answers only from the documents it can see; out-of-date documents give out-of-date answers, so someone owns keeping them current.
