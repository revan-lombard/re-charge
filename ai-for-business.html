What it actually does

Four ways AI earns its place.

Each one is built around your real business: your hours, your prices, your documents, your voice. The AI works from your facts, not guesses.

Answer customers 24/7

A WhatsApp or website assistant that answers the questions you get asked all day — hours, prices, bookings, "do you do X?" — day and night, instantly. It hands the real leads over to you instead of letting them go cold overnight.

Good for: salons, trades, restaurants, guesthouses — anyone losing after-hours enquiries.

Instant answers from your documents

Point AI at your manuals, policies, product lists or price sheets, and your staff (or customers) get a straight answer in seconds instead of digging through files or asking you. Like having someone who's read everything and never forgets.

Good for: businesses with lots of products, procedures or paperwork.

Stop retyping the same things

AI that drafts your quotes, replies, summaries and reports from the details you already have — so a quote that took twenty minutes takes two, and you just check and send.

Good for: anyone sending similar quotes, emails or documents over and over.

Content in your voice

Social posts, product descriptions and email campaigns written in your tone, from a few words of direction — so marketing stops being the job you never get to.

Good for: businesses that know they should post more but never find the time.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to be technical to use AI in my business?

No. We find where AI actually saves you time or wins you customers, build it into a tool you already use (like WhatsApp), and set it up for you. You just use it.

What does AI cost for a small business?

Building it starts from R3,500, with a fixed quote before you pay. Looking after it (maintenance and security) is from R300 a month, or pay yearly and save. The AI usage itself is billed separately at cost, so you always know what you're paying and it's never hidden in the price.

Is my business data safe?

Yes. We only use your information to power your own tool, we don't share it, and we set things up so the AI answers from your real business facts rather than making things up. Keeping it secure is part of the maintenance plan.

Can the AI answer customers on WhatsApp?

Yes. A WhatsApp or website assistant trained on your hours, prices, services and FAQs can answer customers day and night, and hand over to you when it's a real lead.

What if AI isn't right for my business?

Then we'll tell you. The first chat is free and honest: if a simple website, a Google profile or an automation would help you more than AI, we'll point you there instead.
