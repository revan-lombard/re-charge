Questions

Straight answers.

Do we need new software?

Usually not. We connect to what you already use: Microsoft 365 (Outlook, SharePoint, Teams), WhatsApp Business, Xero and spreadsheets directly, and systems like Sage, Pastel, SAP Business One or a practice system through their exports or their API. Already paying for Zapier, Make or Power Automate? We can build on it rather than add something new.

Will it replace our staff?

No. It takes the repetitive part, so your people do the work you hired them for.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Where a mistake would matter, a person approves before anything goes out. We check its accuracy every month.

Is our data safe? Which AI do you use?

It’s used only to run your system, under POPIA, and custom builds run in accounts you own. They use the AI provider named in your project order, usually Claude, through Anthropic’s business service, which doesn’t train its models on your data; its servers are outside South Africa, so that transfer is protected the way POPIA requires. Your quote lists every service your data passes through before we build, and you can sign our data processing agreement first. The Inbox Assistant, and the chat and demos on this site, run on our own AI computer, in South Africa. On the way, each message passes through our Supabase backend, whose servers are outside South Africa, so that transfer is protected the way POPIA requires. Security and data, in one screen
