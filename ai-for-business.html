Questions

Straight answers.

Do we need to change the software we use?

Usually not. We connect to what you already run. If a system has no way in, we’ll tell you in discovery and suggest the least disruptive option.

Will it replace our staff?

It replaces the repetitive part of their work, not their judgement. Most clients use the hours back for the work they hired those people to do. Adoption is one of the six things we score, because a system nobody uses saves nothing.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

We design for it. Where a mistake would cost you, the system prepares and a person approves. We test accuracy before go-live and keep testing it every month in Operations.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays in accounts you own, is used only to run your system, and is handled under POPIA. We tell you which providers process it and where, before anything is built.
