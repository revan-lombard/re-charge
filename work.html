Construction Project Dashboard

A project management dashboard for a contractor running several sites at once. Site managers update progress from their phones; the owner sees every project's status without phoning around.

What it shows

  • Project progress
  • Budget vs actual
  • Tasks and deadlines
  • Resources and crew
  • KPIs per site
  • Weekly summary report

The problem it solves

Progress lives in WhatsApp groups, budgets live in Excel, and the real picture only comes together in someone's head at month-end.
