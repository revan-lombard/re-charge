    How we measure

    Timed before. Timed after. Reported monthly.

    We time the work before we change anything, then the same way after. That’s the number in your report.

    • Hours savedHow long it took before, minus how long it takes now.
    • What they’re worthThose hours, at what the people doing it cost you.
    • When it pays for itselfThe build cost, divided by what it saves each month.

    Example: 3 people spending 5 hours a week on it, with 60% taken over, get about 39 hours a month back.
