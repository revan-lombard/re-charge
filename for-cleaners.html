Questions cleaners ask

Straight answers.

Will the quote match my prices?

Yes. It works only from the rates you give us, and anything unusual is left for you to price.

Can it handle repeat clients?

Yes: reminders and rebooking for weekly and monthly clients, with changes passed to you.

Does it reply by itself?

Only if you want it to. We start with drafts you send in one tap; routine answers can go out by themselves later.

Do I still need a website?

Not to start. A site with an instant quote tool is an add-on from R12,500.

Who will we deal with?

A small studio: the person on your free call builds it and answers your messages, with no call centre or hand-offs. It’s documented and runs in accounts you own, so any developer can pick it up.
