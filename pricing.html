How we work

The rules that keep projects fast and fair.

These apply to every project. They protect your budget as much as our time — and they're why we can keep prices this low.

Fixed scope

Every project has a defined scope before work starts, so both sides know what "done" means.

Fixed price

You get a fixed price for that scope. If the scope holds, the price holds.

Deposit before development

No development begins before the deposit is received and the scope is confirmed.

You own it

Your code, designs, data and domain are yours — move them if you ever leave.

Full terms — including refunds, revisions, delays, ownership and hosting — are on the Terms & Deposit page.

Pricing questions

Common questions about cost.

Why "from" prices instead of a fixed list?

Because a one-page site and a booking site with payments are both "websites", but they aren't the same work. Starting prices tell you roughly where a project lands; the estimate in the Project Builder gets closer, and the final quote is exact.

Is the estimate the final price?

No. The estimate is indicative. We review your project, confirm the scope, and send a fixed quote before any development.

What does a typical first project cost?

Most first projects are a quick build (from R500) or a website (R1,000–R2,500). They're quick to deliver and a low-risk way to see how we work before anything bigger.

Can I start small and add to it later?

Yes — that's the idea. A R1,000 website is an entry point, not the end product. Many clients add a dashboard, automation or a custom tool later, each quoted as its own small project.

Do I have to take Hosting & Care?

No. If you'd rather host and maintain the site yourself or elsewhere, the quote sets out what you need. You always own your domain.

What about AI running costs?

Third-party AI/API usage is billed separately, based on actual usage, and explained before we build. We never bundle unlimited AI usage into a fixed price.
