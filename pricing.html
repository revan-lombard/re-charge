Pricing questions

Common questions about cost.

Why "from" prices instead of a fixed list?

Because a one-page site and a booking site with payments are both "websites", but they aren't the same work. Starting prices tell you roughly where a project lands; the estimate in the Project Builder gets closer, and the final quote is exact.

Is the estimate the final price?

No. The estimate is indicative. I review your project, confirm the scope, and send a fixed quote before any development.

When do I pay the deposit?

Only after you've seen and accepted your fixed quote. I send the quote online; if you're happy, you accept it and pay the deposit by card (Yoco). It comes off your total, and the balance is due on completion. Asking for a quote is free.

Can I pay monthly instead?

Yes, for websites. A one-page site with Care is R249 a month for 12 months, with only the first month to pay upfront, then R150 a month for Care. It costs a little more than paying upfront (R2,988 over the year, against R2,500), and the minimum is 12 months. Your quote can show both, and you pick.

What does a typical first project cost?

Most first projects are an AI assistant (from R5,900) or a one-page site (from R1,000). They're quick to deliver and a low-risk way to see how I work before anything bigger. Minor changes to an existing site start from R250.

Can I start small and add to it later?

Yes — that's the idea. A one-page website is an entry point, not the end product. Many clients add a dashboard, automation or a custom tool later, each quoted as its own small project.

Is hosting required?

Some hosting is needed to keep a site online — but not necessarily mine. Ongoing sites take an annual plan; event or once-off sites only need short-term hosting. You can also host elsewhere or provide your own; you always own your domain.

What about AI running costs?

Third-party AI/API usage is billed separately, based on actual usage, and explained before I build. I never bundle unlimited AI usage into a fixed price.
