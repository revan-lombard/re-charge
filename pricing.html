Pricing questions

Questions.

How does a project work?
  1. Tell us the problem.
  2. Get a fixed quote. Accept it and pay a 40% deposit.
  3. We build, you review.
  4. Pay the balance; it goes live. You own it.
What changes the price?

How much it does, how many systems it connects to, and whether your information needs tidying first.

Can we stop part-way?

Yes, after any step. If the small version doesn’t save enough time, we’ll say so and you stop there.

Why a monthly fee?

AI tools drift and the services they use change. The fee keeps it accurate, fixed and improving, and shows you the time saved.

Do you build websites?

Yes: from R12,500, or R35,000 with bookings or payments.

Isn’t this too expensive for a business our size?

Start small: the Inbox Assistant is set up for you first, and you pay (R9,500 setup, then R2,950 a month) only once it’s saving 5 hours a week. Use the calculator to see what that work costs you now. If the saving won’t cover the price, we’ll tell you on the free call, before you spend anything.

We already have a system. Why change?

You may not need to. We usually build on what you already use and connect it, rather than replace it. If your system already does the job, we’ll say so.

What if the AI gets it wrong?

A person checks anything that matters before it goes out, and answers show where they came from. The trial is measured on your real work, and if it misses the target we agreed, you get half back.

Will it replace my staff?

It takes the repetitive part: the typing, copying and the same answers every day. Your people keep the judgement, the customers and the decisions, with more time for them.

What happens to our data?

It’s used only to run your system, in accounts you own where possible, under POPIA. You own the system and the data. Privacy

Can I get information first, without a call?

Yes. Work out your numbers in the calculator and email them to yourself, with what fixing it would cost. Or . We follow up at most once, and never chase.

Now isn’t a good time. Maybe next year?

That’s fine. Email yourself the estimate so you have the numbers when you plan. When you’re ready, the free call is still free.

We’ve never worked with you. Why should we trust you?

You don’t have to take it on trust. The price is fixed before we start, you pay 40% up front, you can stop after any stage, the trial has a half-back guarantee, and you own what we build.

Have a question before committing?

Ask it. Tell us what you’re trying to improve and we’ll tell you honestly what it would cost, including the ongoing part.
