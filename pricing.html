Pricing questions

Common questions about cost.

How does a project work?
  1. Tell us the problem in your own words.
  2. We propose a solution: the simplest thing that works, which isn’t always AI.
  3. You get a fixed proposal. Approve it and pay a 40% deposit.
  4. We build it and you review it. Agreed changes within the scope are included.
  5. Pay the balance and we launch. You own it.

Deposits, revisions, payment, ownership and handover are in the terms.

What affects the cost?
  • Scope: how many pages, screens, steps or users.
  • Integrations: how many systems it connects to, and whether they have a way in.
  • Complexity: the rules, exceptions and approvals it has to handle.
  • Data: whether your information is ready to use or needs cleaning first.
  • Ongoing needs: how much support, monitoring and change you want afterwards.
What do I get at the starting price?

A well-defined first project: a business website, a dashboard from your main systems, one automated workflow, one focused AI tool, or a focused custom tool. More pages, sources, integrations or users add to the price, and your proposal shows exactly what’s included.

Why doesn’t every project cost the starting price?

Because a one-page site and a booking system with payments are both “websites”, but they’re not the same work. Starting prices tell you where a project begins; the fixed proposal tells you what yours costs, before anything is built.

Why isn’t discovery free?

Because it’s the most valuable part: a map of your processes, measured baselines and a scored list of opportunities. You keep it whether or not you build with us, and it’s credited in full if you do within 60 days. The process review before it is free.

Can we stop after the proof of concept?

Yes. That’s what it’s for. If the measured result doesn’t justify production, we’ll say so, and you’ve only paid for the proof of concept.

Why is Operations a monthly fee?

AI systems aren’t finished at go-live. Models change, your processes change, and accuracy drifts if nobody checks it. Operations covers the model and API costs, monitoring, security, evaluation, support and improvements, and proves the value every month.

What if our AI usage goes above the allowance?

We warn you at 80% of the allowance. Anything above it is billed at cost, never marked up. If it keeps happening, we’ll suggest the plan or design change that brings it down.

Do you build websites?

Yes: business websites from R12,500 and platforms from R35,000, with a fixed proposal first, plus managed hosting or Care. They’re priced and run separately from the AI work.

Have a question before committing?

Ask it. Tell us what you’re trying to improve and we’ll tell you honestly what it would cost, including the ongoing part.
