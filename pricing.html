How we work

The rules that keep projects fast and fair.

These apply to every Re-Charge project. They protect your budget as much as they protect our time.

Fixed scope

Every project receives a defined scope before work starts, so both sides know exactly what "done" means.

Fixed price

You receive a fixed project price based on that scope. If the scope holds, the price holds.

Defined revision rounds

Each quote includes a set number of revision rounds. Feedback is gathered per round so changes stay organised and the project keeps moving.

Deposit before development

No significant development begins before the agreed deposit has been received. The deposit confirms the project and reserves the time to build it.

Change requests

Anything outside the original scope becomes a change request. We'll tell you whether it costs extra before doing it, and you decide.

Third-party services

Hosting, domains, API usage, paid software, AI consumption and other third-party costs are clearly identified and either paid directly by you or included as a clearly defined additional cost.

Ownership

Your quotation clearly defines who owns the source code, designs, client data, third-party components, licences and AI/API accounts, and which reusable Re-Charge components remain ours.

Direct communication

You deal directly with the person building your solution. Questions get answered by someone who knows the answer.

Pricing questions

Common questions about cost.

Why starting prices instead of a price list?

Because a five-page brochure site and a booking site with payments are both "websites", but they aren't the same amount of work. Starting prices tell you roughly where a project lands. The fixed quote tells you exactly.

Is the quote really fixed?

Yes, for the scope it describes. If you ask for something outside that scope, it's handled as a change request with its own price, and you decide whether to proceed. The original price never changes on its own.

What does a typical first project cost?

Most first projects sit between R2,500 and R7,500: a small website, a landing page, a dashboard, a small automation or a simple integration. They're quick to deliver and a low-risk way to see how we work.

Can I start small and add to it later?

That's usually the best approach. We'll build the smallest version that solves the problem, and structure it so the next features can be added as separate, quoted pieces of work.

Do I have to take a support plan?

No. Support is optional. If you'd rather host and maintain the solution yourself, or with someone else, the quotation will set out what you need to do that.
