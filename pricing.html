Pricing questions

Common questions about cost.

Why isn’t discovery free?

Because it’s the most valuable part: a map of your processes, measured baselines and a scored list of opportunities. You keep it whether or not you build with us, and it’s credited in full if you do within 60 days. The process review before it is free.

Can we stop after the proof of concept?

Yes. That’s what it’s for. If the measured result doesn’t justify production, we’ll say so, and you’ve only paid for the proof of concept.

Why is Operations a monthly fee?

AI systems aren’t finished at go-live. Models change, your processes change, and accuracy drifts if nobody checks it. Operations covers the model and API costs, monitoring, security, evaluation, support and improvements, and proves the value every month.

What if our AI usage goes above the allowance?

We warn you at 80% of the allowance. Anything above it is billed at cost, never marked up. If it keeps happening, we’ll suggest the plan or design change that brings it down.

Do you still build websites?

Yes, from R1,000 with a free preview first, plus Hosting and Care plans. They’re priced and run separately from the AI work.
