Pricing questions

Common questions about cost.

Why "from" prices instead of a fixed list?

Because a one-page site and a booking site with payments are both "websites", but they aren't the same work. Starting prices tell you roughly where a project lands; the estimate in the Project Builder gets closer, and the final quote is exact.

Is the estimate the final price?

No. The estimate is indicative. We review your project, confirm the scope, and send a fixed quote before any development.

What does a typical first project cost?

Most first projects are a quick build or one-page site (from R1,000). They're quick to deliver and a low-risk way to see how we work before anything bigger. Minor changes to an existing site start from R250.

Can I start small and add to it later?

Yes — that's the idea. A R1,000 website is an entry point, not the end product. Many clients add a dashboard, automation or a custom tool later, each quoted as its own small project.

Is hosting required?

Some hosting is needed to keep a site online — but not necessarily ours. Ongoing sites take an annual plan; event or once-off sites only need short-term hosting. You can also host elsewhere or provide your own; you always own your domain.

What about AI running costs?

Third-party AI/API usage is billed separately, based on actual usage, and explained before we build. We never bundle unlimited AI usage into a fixed price.
