Dashboards

Turn your business data into something you can actually understand. Built from the spreadsheets and systems you already have, so the numbers you care about are one screen away.

Instead of someone compiling figures by hand, the dashboard reads the source data, does the maths and shows the result. Reports can be generated and sent automatically.

Examples

  • Business dashboards
  • Sales dashboards
  • Financial dashboards
  • Project dashboards
  • Reporting dashboards
  • Excel / CSV dashboards
  • Management dashboards

Good fit if

  • Someone rebuilds the same report every week or month.
  • Your data lives in several spreadsheets that never quite agree.
  • You want to see how the business is doing without asking for the numbers.

Starting price

FromR2,000

Depends on how many data sources are involved and how much cleaning the data needs.

Bring your data

Excel, CSV or an export is enough to start. You can attach a sample file in the Project Builder.

Automation

Remove repetitive manual work. If a task follows the same steps every time, it can almost always be automated.

Instead of spending an hour every day copying orders into a spreadsheet, we build a workflow that does it — and sends the confirmation — without anyone lifting a finger.

Examples

  • Automated reports
  • Form → database workflows
  • Email automation
  • Document generation
  • Data processing
  • Repetitive admin
  • Business process automation

Good fit if

  • Information gets copied between systems by hand.
  • Customers wait for confirmations, quotes or documents that follow a standard format.
  • A process only works because one person remembers all the steps.

Starting price

FromR2,000

Small single-workflow automations sit near the start. Multi-system integrations are quoted on scope.

Quick win

Automations are often the fastest way to get real time back. Show us the process and we'll estimate it.

AI Integrations

Practical AI added to a real business process — never AI for its own sake. We'll tell you honestly where it adds value, and where it doesn't.

The most useful AI projects are usually simple: an assistant that answers from your own documents, a search that understands what people mean, or a step in a workflow that used to need a person to read and summarise.

Examples

  • AI assistants
  • Business knowledge assistants
  • Document Q&A
  • AI-powered search
  • AI content generation
  • AI classification
  • AI customer support
  • AI workflow automation

Good fit if

  • The same questions get asked again and again, and the answers are written down somewhere.
  • People spend time reading, sorting or summarising documents.
  • You want to use AI but need someone to find where it actually helps.

Usage is billed separately. Third-party AI/API usage is charged based on actual use, and we explain it before building. We never bundle unlimited AI into a fixed price.
